This policy explains how Mosi O Tunya Car Care handles personal data when you visit this website, contact us or use our services, and the rights you have under Zambia's Data Protection Act, 2021.

Who we are

This website, and the services described on it, are operated by Mosi-o-Tunya Car Care Services Limited, trading as Mosi O Tunya Car Care ("we", "us", "our"), of Plot 25313, Mosi-oa-Tunya Road, Woodlands, Lusaka, Zambia. We are the data controller for the personal data described in this policy.

This policy explains what personal data we collect, how we use and protect it, who we share it with, and the rights you have under the laws of Zambia, including the Data Protection Act, No. 3 of 2021 (the "Act").

What personal data we collect

This website does not have contact forms, user accounts, a booking system or a payment system, and it does not require you to enter any personal data to browse it. We collect personal data in the following ways:

Data you give us directly

  • When you contact us by phone, WhatsApp, email or in person: your name, phone number, email address, the content of your message and any details you give us about your vehicle or premises (for example, registration number, make and model, or the address for a mobile call-out).
  • When you use our services: your contact details, vehicle details, the services requested, service dates and payment records. For fleet and corporate clients we keep service logs and the details of a nominated contact person.
  • For car sales agency services: the details reasonably needed to list, market or transfer a vehicle, such as ownership and registration documents.

Data collected automatically

  • Server logs. Like most websites, the server that hosts this site may automatically record technical information about each visit, such as your IP address, browser type, the pages requested and the date and time of the request. We use this only to keep the site running and secure.
  • Third-party requests. When a page loads, your browser requests our web fonts from Google Fonts, and, only if you click "Show map" on the Contact page, the Google Maps embed. These requests send your IP address to Google. See our Cookie Policy for details.

We do not use analytics, advertising or social-media tracking tools on this website, and this website does not set cookies of its own.

How we use your personal data

We use personal data only for the following purposes and on the following legal bases under the Act:

Purposes of processing and legal bases
PurposeLegal basis
Responding to your enquiries and quotationsSteps taken at your request before entering into a contract; our legitimate interest in responding to people who contact us

We do not sell personal data, and we do not use automated decision-making or profiling.

Who we share your data with

We share personal data only where necessary, and only with:

  • Service providers that help us run our business, such as our website hosting provider, email provider and messaging platforms (for example, WhatsApp, operated by Meta Platforms, when you choose to message us through it).
  • Google, which receives your IP address when your browser loads our web fonts or, on request, the embedded map. Google's processing is governed by its own privacy policy.
  • Professional advisers such as accountants, auditors and lawyers, where needed.
  • Public authorities where the law requires it, for example the Zambia Revenue Authority or law enforcement acting under a lawful request.
  • Buyers, sellers and licensing authorities in connection with car sales agency services, to the extent needed to complete a transaction you have asked us to handle.

International transfers

Our hosting, email and messaging providers may store or process data on servers outside Zambia. Where personal data leaves Zambia we rely on the conditions permitted by the Act, including your consent where required, and on the safeguards offered by those providers.

How long we keep your data

  • Enquiries that do not lead to a service: kept for up to 12 months, then deleted.
  • Service, invoicing and payment records: kept for as long as required by Zambian tax and company law after the last transaction, then deleted or anonymised.
  • Fleet and corporate contracts: kept for the duration of the contract and for the statutory period after it ends.
  • Server logs: retained by our hosting provider for a short rolling period for security purposes.

How we protect your data

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect personal data against loss, misuse, unauthorised access, disclosure or alteration. These include serving this website over HTTPS, limiting access to customer records to staff who need them, and using password-protected devices and accounts.

No method of transmission over the internet or of electronic storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security. If we become aware of a personal-data breach that is likely to affect your rights, we will notify the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner and, where required, you, in line with the Act.

Your rights

Under the Data Protection Act, 2021, you have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you and be told how we use it;
  • Rectification of inaccurate or incomplete data;
  • Erasure of your data where there is no longer a lawful reason for us to keep it;
  • Restrict or object to processing, including any direct marketing;
  • Data portability: receive the data you gave us in a commonly used, machine-readable format;
  • Withdraw consent at any time where processing is based on consent, without affecting processing already carried out;
  • Lodge a complaint with the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner if you believe we have processed your data unlawfully.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the details below. We may ask you to confirm your identity before acting on a request, and we will respond within the time allowed by the Act. Exercising your rights is free of charge, except where the Act permits a reasonable fee for additional copies.

Children

Our services are intended for adults. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has given us personal data, please contact us and we will delete it.

Links to other websites

This website links to third-party sites and services, including Facebook, Instagram, WhatsApp and Google Maps. We are not responsible for their content or privacy practices. Please read their privacy policies before sharing personal data with them.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top of the page shows when it was last changed. Significant changes will be highlighted on this website.
