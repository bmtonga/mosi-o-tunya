01

Car Wash & Detailing

Every wash at Mosi O Tunya starts the same way: by hand, with clean water, pH-balanced products and separate mitts for paintwork and wheels. No automated brushes, no shortcuts. Whether you want a fast refresh before a meeting or a complete detail that resets the car inside and out, the standard of care is the same.

Choose from four packages, Essential Wash, Premium Wash, Interior Refresh and Full Detail, or ask for our Executive Care Package when the car deserves everything. Final pricing depends on vehicle size and condition and is confirmed before any work begins.

What's included

  • Pre-rinse and snow-foam to lift grit before contact
  • Two-bucket hand wash of all exterior panels
  • Wheel, tyre and arch cleaning with tyre dressing
  • Door shuts, sills and fuel flap cleaned
  • Interior vacuum, dash and console wipe-down
  • Glass cleaned inside and out
  • Wax or sealant and machine polish (Full Detail and above)
  • Leather and upholstery care (Executive Care Package)

How it works

  1. Arrive or book ahead

    Drive in during opening hours, or message us on WhatsApp to reserve a slot and tell us about the car.

  2. Agree the package

    We look at the vehicle with you, recommend a package and confirm the price before starting.

  3. We work; you relax

    Wait in comfort or leave the car with us. Allow roughly an hour for washes and half a day or more for a Full Detail.

  4. Final inspection

    We walk the car with you before handover so nothing is missed.

Good to know

  • Packages and indicative prices are listed on the Packages section; the final price is confirmed on arrival.
  • Heavily soiled vehicles, pet hair and stain removal may need extra time and are quoted separately.
  • We can collect and return your vehicle in Lusaka by arrangement; see Mobile Car Wash & Call-Outs.
