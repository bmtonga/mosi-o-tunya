This policy describes the cookies and similar technologies actually used on the Mosi O Tunya Car Care website, based on a review of every page and script, and how you can control them.

What cookies are

Cookies are small text files that a website stores on your device when you visit it. They let the site remember things about your visit, such as settings or whether you are logged in. Similar technologies include local storage, pixels and embedded content from other websites, which can also record information about you.

Cookies set by this website

We reviewed every page and script on this website in preparing this policy. The result is simple:

This website does not set any cookies of its own, and it does not use analytics, advertising or social-media tracking. The only thing it stores in your browser is your cookie choice itself.

How to manage cookies

  • Cookie Settings. Use the link in the footer of any page to change your choice at any time. Optional content is off until you allow it.
  • The map. The Google Maps embed is never loaded automatically. It loads only if you have allowed "External content (Google Maps)" and click "Show map"; the address and the "Get directions" link work either way.
  • Browser settings. You can block or delete cookies, including third-party cookies, in your browser's privacy settings. Blocking all cookies will not affect this website, as it does not rely on any.
  • Fonts. If your browser blocks requests to Google, the site falls back to a standard system typeface and remains fully usable.

Guidance for common browsers: Chrome, Firefox, Safari and Microsoft Edge.

Changes to this policy

We will update this page if the cookies or third-party services used on the website change. The date at the top of the page shows when it was last reviewed.
