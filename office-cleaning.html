04

Office Cleaning Services

The same discipline we bring to vehicles applies to workplaces: a defined scope, trained staff, the right products for each surface, and a checklist signed off at the end of every visit. We clean offices, showrooms, clinics, shops and shared commercial spaces across Lusaka.

Choose the frequency that fits your business. Many clients prefer early-morning or after-hours visits so that cleaning never interrupts the working day.

What's included

  • Floors swept, vacuumed and mopped
  • Desks, surfaces and touch-points wiped and sanitised
  • Interior glass, partitions and doors cleaned
  • Kitchens and break areas cleaned
  • Washrooms cleaned and restocked (consumables by arrangement)
  • Bins emptied and waste removed to your collection point
  • Periodic deep cleans on request
  • Carpet cleaning available as an add-on

How it works

  1. Walk-through

    We visit your premises to understand the space and your expectations.

  2. Scope and schedule

    You receive a written scope, frequency and price.

  3. Cleaning begins

    Our team works to the checklist at the agreed times.

  4. Sign-off and review

    A supervisor checks each visit; we review the scope with you regularly.

Good to know

  • Pricing depends on floor area, frequency and scope; quotes are free.
  • Cleaning materials are included; consumables such as hand soap and tissue can be supplied on request.
  • After-hours and weekend cleaning is available by arrangement.
