Fleet Washing Contracts

A clean fleet says something about the organisation that runs it. Our fleet contracts replace ad-hoc washing with a planned programme: agreed vehicles, agreed frequency, agreed standard, and a service log that shows exactly what was done and when.

We work with company car pools, car-hire businesses, NGOs, delivery fleets and institutions. Washing can happen at our Woodlands facility, at your yard or depot, or a mix of both, and invoicing is consolidated monthly where agreed.

What's included

  • Scheduled washes for an agreed number of vehicles
  • Exterior wash, wheels and tyres as standard
  • Interior vacuum and wipe-down for passenger vehicles
  • Service log per vehicle (date, registration, work done)
  • Dedicated contract contact for your fleet manager
  • Monthly consolidated invoicing where agreed
  • On-site washing at your depot by arrangement
  • Periodic detailing or valeting as an add-on

How it works

  1. Tell us about the fleet

    Vehicle types and count, where they are based, and how often they should be cleaned.

  2. Site visit and proposal

    We visit if needed, then send a written proposal with scope, schedule and pricing.

  3. Programme starts

    Washing runs to the agreed schedule, with a log updated after every visit.

  4. Monthly review

    One invoice, one report, and a review of anything you would like changed.

Good to know

  • Contract pricing depends on vehicle count, frequency and location; there is no fixed rate card.
  • Minimum contract terms and payment terms are agreed in writing before work starts.
  • Our Corporate Vehicle Care package summarises the standard inclusions.
