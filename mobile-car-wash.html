Mobile Car Wash & Call-Outs

Not everyone has time to drive to Woodlands. Our mobile team brings the wash to you, with our own water, power, products and equipment, so all you need to provide is a parking space. It suits busy professionals, residential estates, hotels and offices with several vehicles to clean at once.

Call-outs are scheduled in advance so that we can plan water and travel. Tell us where you are, how many vehicles need attention and which package you would like, and we will confirm a time and price.

What's included

  • Exterior hand wash with our own water supply and equipment
  • Wheels, tyres and arches cleaned and dressed
  • Interior vacuum and wipe-down
  • Glass inside and out
  • Waterless or rinseless wash options for restricted sites
  • Multiple vehicles at one location on the same visit
  • Waxing and interior detailing on request
  • Wastewater contained and removed where required

How it works

  1. Send us your location

    Message us on WhatsApp with your address, the number of vehicles and the package you want.

  2. We confirm time and price

    Call-out charges depend on distance and vehicle count; we confirm the total before we travel.

  3. We arrive and set up

    Our team arrives with everything needed and works around your schedule.

  4. Inspection and payment

    You inspect the vehicles with us; pay by cash or mobile money on completion.

Good to know

  • Coverage is Lusaka and surrounding areas; distance affects the call-out charge.
  • Access to a tap or power can reduce the cost but is not required.
  • Mobile work is subject to weather and booking availability; we will always confirm the day before.
