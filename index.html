Who we are

Operating under Mosi-o-Tunya Properties and Car Care Services Limited, we serve individual motorists, corporate clients and fleet operators through on-site washing, detailing, mobile call-outs and complementary cleaning services for homes and workplaces.

Our approach is simple: respect every vehicle, value every customer and deliver dependable service that encourages long-term relationships.

Our vision

To become a trusted and recognisable car-care brand in Lusaka, known for quality, convenience and professionalism.

Our mission

To provide accessible, high-quality vehicle and cleaning services through trained staff, clear service standards and customer-focused operations.

Our promise: a cleaner vehicle, a better experience. Clean results. Careful service. A finish worth returning for.
