About us

Professional vehicle care, delivered with consistency and pride.

Mosi O Tunya Car Care is a vehicle-care facility at Plot 25313, Mosi-oa-Tunya Road, Woodlands, Lusaka. Our aim is simple: make car care convenient, reliable and professional in a clean, well-organised environment.

Operating under Mosi-o-Tunya Properties and Car Care Services Limited, we serve individual motorists, corporate clients and fleet operators through on-site washing, detailing, mobile call-outs and complementary cleaning services.

  • Paved, shaded wash bays
  • Trained, customer-focused team
  • Secure, fenced premises
  • Clear scope and pricing
  • Responsible water use
  • Lounge & café while you wait
Our promise: a cleaner vehicle, a better experience. Respect every vehicle. Value every customer. Deliver dependable service that earns long-term trust.
