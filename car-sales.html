06

Car Sales Agency

Selling a car privately takes time: photographs, listings, calls, viewings and paperwork. Our sales agency handles it for you. Because we are a car-care business first, every vehicle we list is detailed before it is photographed, which is the single biggest difference in the price a car achieves.

We agree an asking price and a commission with you up front, manage all enquiries and viewings, and keep you informed at every step until the sale is completed and the vehicle is handed over.

What's included

  • Honest valuation and pricing advice
  • Full detail before photography
  • Professional photographs and a written listing
  • Advertising through our channels and partner platforms
  • Enquiries, viewings and test drives handled by us
  • Buyer vetting and negotiation on your behalf
  • Guidance on transfer paperwork
  • Secure handover of vehicle and documents

How it works

  1. Bring the car in

    We inspect the vehicle, check the documents and agree the asking price and commission in writing.

  2. Preparation

    The car is detailed and photographed at our facility.

  3. Marketing and viewings

    We list the vehicle, handle every enquiry and arrange viewings.

  4. Sale and handover

    We manage the negotiation, payment and paperwork, and hand over to the buyer.

Good to know

  • Commission and any preparation costs are agreed in writing before the vehicle is listed.
  • We only list vehicles with clear ownership documents.
  • Vehicles can be stored at our facility during the sale by arrangement.
